OLBERG CONSULTING — BRAND KIT
==============================

Ready-made files for use outside the website (LinkedIn, email, documents).
The website itself does not need this folder, except for olberg-mark.png, which
the email signature loads from your published site.

LinkedIn
  linkedin-profile.png     400 x 400   Profile / company page picture (the round mark)
  linkedin-banner.jpg     1584 x 396   Background banner; the left side is kept clear
                                       because LinkedIn places your profile photo there

Documents, proposals, reports, slides, invoices (transparent background)
  logo-olberg.png         2400 x 697   Full logo for white / light backgrounds
  logo-olberg-light.png   2400 x 697   Full logo for dark backgrounds
  olberg-mark.png         1024 x 1024  Stand-alone mark for light backgrounds
  olberg-mark-light.png   1024 x 1024  Stand-alone mark for dark backgrounds

  Vector (scalable) originals are in the website's images folder:
  images/logo-olberg.svg, images/logo-olberg-light.svg,
  images/olberg-mark.svg, images/olberg-mark-light.svg

Email signature
  email-signature.html    Open it, follow the instructions at the top, copy & paste
                          into Outlook or Gmail.

Brand colours
  Deep Ocean Blue  #0F4C81   headings, logo
  Turquoise        #00A6A6   accents, water
  Lime Green       #84CC16   highlights, buttons (use sparingly)
  Dark navy        #061F36   dark backgrounds

Fonts (free, SIL Open Font License)
  Space Grotesk — headings · Inter — body text · IBM Plex Mono — small technical labels
